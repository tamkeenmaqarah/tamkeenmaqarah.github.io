## Overview

خيمة ياسين is the Sunday-morning circle inside Tamkeen. The public site is an operating tool: open a quiz, see the standings, read a teacher-approved mix-up. The tent is the masthead and the two dark panels, not a costume on every control.

The cloth is the reference. Vertical crimson stripes, a black pixel frieze along the hem, brass for the champion and for the action that sits on the cloth. The working surface is ivory so questions, names, and scores stay readable. Arabic leads the name. English runs the tasks.

## Colors

Tent red `#9d1c2b` and tent deep `#7a1420` are the stripe pair. Tent night `#1a1214` is the narrow dark stripe and the ground of the pixel hem. Ivory `#fffdf8` is the sheet. Canvas `#f7f1e6` is the page. Sand `#f6e7d4` is the quiet fill behind notes and second-place steps. Brass `#d7b56a` marks first place and the button on a striped panel. Ink `#241416` is body text. Muted `#6d534c` is secondary text. Both clear 4.5:1 on canvas.

Green is reserved for a saved grade, a published quiz, and a correct review mark. It is not the brand.

## Typography

Reem Kufi carries the Arabic name only: the masthead الخيمة and the home title خيمة ياسين. Figtree carries navigation, questions, and instructions. Barlow Condensed carries scores. All three are self-hosted. Do not load a font from a third-party stylesheet.

## Layout

The page is a 1120px column. The header is sticky: brand, teacher sign-in, then a single row of text buttons. On a narrow screen the student navigation becomes four equal columns and the home title, Sunday card, and quiz panel stack. The home title stays large enough to read as the name of the place.

## Elevation & Depth

Surfaces are flat. The tent panels are cloth, not cards with drop shadows. The pixel hem is the only edge treatment on those panels. Focus is a 3px brass ring.

## Shapes

Sheets and panels use 14px corners. Controls use 8px. The brand mark is a squared pixel block, matching the hem, not a circle and not a school icon.

## Components

The striped panel is the signature. It is the header, the Sunday card, and the open-quiz band. Each one ends in the same 16px pixel frieze. Do not put stripes behind a paragraph of instructions.

The leaderboard podium is a solid tent-deep panel. First place is brass. Second place is ivory. Third place is a dark brown step with ivory numerals. Equal scores share a rank. The champion row is a brass wash, not a new color.

Primary buttons on ivory are tent red with ivory text. The button on a striped panel is brass with tent-deep text. Secondary buttons are ivory with a sand border.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do keep the historical scores and the published mix-up as records. Do not invent students, attendance, or streaks.

Do not return to the navy league, a green mosque panel, or a cream-and-terracotta editorial theme. Do not use the stock photograph of children in a tent. Draw the cloth with the stripe and the pixel hem.

Do not add eyebrow labels, kickers, or a second decorative pattern. One signature is enough.
