# Optional Gemini study aids

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing Najdi working surfaces without replacing their visual system.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Offer a small, deliberate aid beside existing assignments, halaqa notes and missed-answer teacher notes.
OWN-WORLD: Native study disclosures use the established script pairing, brass book icon, warm rules, visible Gemini label, cream fields and amber actions. Revision answers open on a quiet paper-red inset. The three-phase plan uses a ruled horizontal timeline and small amber diamonds; below 750px it becomes vertical.
STORY: Explicitly create a Recall/Check/Repeat plan for 5, 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes; create three cards from 100–6,000 characters of supplied halaqa notes; or explain an existing teacher note on a missed answer. Disclose submitted homework/notes before activation. Keep suggestions attributed, source checked and separate from scores, assigned material and teacher recitation assessment. Card answers remain exact source excerpts; never invent religious texts or rulings.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A collapsed tool beside its source material. Opening reveals one short form with readable status/error feedback. Halaqas retain the selected Saturday series and Tuesday playlist PLJHIkqUYcGAA, The Arab Spring, plus the existing activated player and external YouTube fallback.
FORM: Browser requests go to Cloudflare, never to Gemini with a client key. New plan/card limits are eight per account or unsigned network daily and forty shared. Source drafts live in session storage; generated-result reuse is bounded to forty in-memory entries. Language and source changes separate results; stale requests abort. No automatic generation or paid voice correction.
FINISH: Extracted from study-tools.js, components.js and styles.css plus server learning validation. Deployment verification on 28 October 2026 confirmed three exact-excerpt cards in English and Arabic and an Arabic three-phase plan totaling 15 minutes. Static frontend publication is tracked separately.

## Motion thesis

Opening a study body uses 230ms opacity/translate settling with the existing ease-out curve. The chevron turns over 160ms. Reduced motion removes both. The plan, native answer disclosures and controls stay usable without motion.
