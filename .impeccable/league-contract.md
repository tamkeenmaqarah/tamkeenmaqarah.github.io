# Whole-site redesign

Mode: Operate. Targets: quiz library, student attempts, results/standings, teacher library, quiz builder, login, loading/errors/empty states. User delegates selection to the assistant and chooses code-first. All product capabilities and private-grade boundaries stay intact.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The Tamkeen League is a friendly youth quiz league. The useful quiz list and real competition replace the former welcome billboard and interchangeable rounded cards.

OWN-WORLD: Navy club masthead, vivid royal blue actions, yellow champion podium, pale blue working surfaces, coral and lavender rank accents. Self-hosted Figtree for readable UI and Barlow Condensed for scoreboard numerals. Crisp 12px corners, flat task rows and strong baseline alignment.

STORY: Students immediately find an open quiz, answer one question at a time and review a real score. The teacher sees the original tied standings, manages quiz availability and builds new editions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact club masthead and three explicit tabs. A left quiz lane contains the immediate action; a right navy standings lane contains a real ranked podium for authorized teachers. No invented activity or progress. On phones the content is one column with a compact three-tab bar. Signature interaction: question changes use one short horizontal arrival; submitted score gets a finite victory burst. Reduced motion removes both.

FORM: Youth tournament scoreboard and team sheets; grounded candidate 1, assistant’s pick delegated by the user; seed 71a555e0. Other candidates were game console, trip passport, community poster, club magazine, museum exhibit and route map. This direction’s honest risk is familiar scoreboard language; precise typography and meaningful task density carry its distinction.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
